Silicon Valley · Boardroom Reversal

Huang Is Back at Nvidia

Days after moving to replace him, Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO — reversing itself after lobbying by President Trump, who feared Huang might go to China.

Shares leapt about 31% on Monday's news, and markets ripped higher into Tuesday's close: S&P 500 +4.73%, Nasdaq +6.51%, Nikkei +3.91%.

Middle East · Hormuz

Three Tankers Strike Mines in Hormuz

Iran's Revolutionary Guard says three oil tankers hit mines despite a U.S. escort. Tehran rules out talks until seven conditions are met; Washington sanctions two dozen Iranian exchange houses.

War at Sea · Libya

Ukraine Sinks Russian Warship off Libya

Ukrainian naval drones struck a Russian warship and a cargo vessel in Benghazi port, taking the war to the Mediterranean. Libya condemned the attack and demanded an investigation.

Space · China

Shenzhou-24 Carries a First to Orbit

China launched Shenzhou-24 to the Tiangong station with two Chinese astronauts and Zeeshan Ali — the first Pakistani astronaut to board the station.

World Court · Myanmar

14 Nations Back Genocide Ruling

The UK, Spain, Canada, Australia and ten others affirmed the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya. Britain sanctioned five generals.

Climate · COP31

COP31 Ends Without a Deal

The climate summit closed with no final text, after the EU and UK rejected a draft that omitted any fossil-fuel phase-out.

Sport · Africa Cup

Senegal Restored as Africa Champions

The Court of Arbitration for Sport reinstated Senegal as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners, overturning the ruling that had handed the title to Morocco.
